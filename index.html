    What is included, and how to read it

    Scope

    A curated set of primary studies of urine volatile organic compounds (VOCs) and electronic noses for prostate-cancer research, including sample-preparation and drift studies. This is a starting collection, not a systematic review. Publication counts are not counts of independent cohorts.

    Every record links to the original paper and identifies the sections used. “Not extracted” means this tool has no verified value; it does not mean the authors failed to report it.

    Check PubMed for newer studies

    Validation labels

    Cross-validation: repeated training and testing within a cohort. Same-cohort holdout: one recruited cohort split into training and test groups. Blinded test: predictions tested with labels concealed; see the record for separation from development data. Separate test cohort: an additional cohort reported by the paper; this does not establish independent, multi-centre validation.

    Study populations, devices and endpoints differ. Reported performance belongs to the specified analysis; it is not a league table or a prediction of Anemo’s performance.

    Use and privacy

    For literature review and research planning. This tool does not diagnose cancer or prescribe a sampling protocol. Check original methods and local approvals before using them in a study.

    No sign-in. Selections and notes stay in this browser. Shared links include study IDs and filters, never your notes. Downloads contain your notes when selected.
