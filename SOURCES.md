# Source and extraction register

Reviewed: 21 November 2026. Curated selection, not a systematic review.

Each record retains its source coverage and source-section locators. Not extracted means unverified here, not necessarily unreported. Validation labels describe the extracted methods; a holdout from one recruited cohort is distinguished from an additional cohort. Counts are whole-study people unless the row explicitly identifies the disease-comparison subset. Do not sum across papers: overlap is unresolved.

No full papers, abstracts or patient-level data are redistributed. Briefs use concise factual paraphrases. Original publications remain authoritative.

## Additional interpretation notes

- Roine: participant and specimen totals are calculated from the paper’s 50 cancer patients, 15 BPH patients and 24 BPH specimens.
- Aggio: 131 = 58 prostate cancers + 73 controls; 24 bladder cancers contribute to the overall total of 155.
- Gao: 183 is the sum of stated development and test-set participants. The test-set recruitment independence is not established by the accessed abstract.
- Lima: the paper uses the word external, but its Subjects and Statistical analysis sections describe a random split of one recruited cohort.
- Taverna: a double-blinded post-training evaluation is described. Recruitment independence from development is not established in this extraction.
- Bax: year follows the January 2022 issue; online publication was 16 December 2021.
- Piyarom: controls combine BPH and normal subjects. The model includes PSA and was evaluated without resampling.
